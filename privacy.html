Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sellery Brands (“Sellery,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, company name, website, company type, revenue range, the nature of your inquiry, and any information you include in your message. We may also receive basic technical information such as the page used to submit the form and standard server logs.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review and respond to inquiries, evaluate consulting, partnership, investment, technology, or acquisition opportunities, maintain a record of the conversation, protect the website from abuse, and improve our operations.

PDF submissions and email delivery

The contact form creates a PDF record of the information you submit and sends that PDF to info@sellerydigital.com. Transactional email infrastructure may process the message and attachment on our behalf.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that support hosting, security, email delivery, legal compliance, or business operations, and with professional advisers when appropriate. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or systems.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including opportunity review, recordkeeping, legal, and security needs. No system can guarantee absolute security, but we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. Contact us at info@sellerydigital.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as our website, services, or legal obligations change. The date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to info@sellerydigital.com.
